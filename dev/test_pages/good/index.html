The idea

A toy relationship: bar i has height a × i. Optionally the bars are normalized so they sum to one.

Why it matters

It gives the checker a page where every control visibly changes the picture.

Symbols

ascale factor
nnumber of bars
hiheight of bar i

How it works

  1. Compute hi = a × i for i = 1..n.
  2. If normalizing, divide each hi by their sum.
  3. Draw one bar per value.

Guided explorations

Exploration 1: more bars

Change n to 8. Observe the extra bars. Why: one bar per value.

Exploration 2: normalize

Tick normalize. Observe the sum become 1. Why: each bar is divided by the total.

Limitation

The relationship is linear by construction; real systems rarely are.
